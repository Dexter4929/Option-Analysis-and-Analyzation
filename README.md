# Stock Options Flow & Sentiment Visualizer

A Python-based data visualization dashboard that pulls live stock options chains and intraday price data via the Yahoo Finance API. Designed to bridge the gap between short-term intraday momentum and long-term expiration positioning on a single screen for faster strategy planning and decision-making.

A small project to assist me on my computer science educational journey. 

⚠️ Disclaimer
This tool is for educational, research, and visualization purposes only. Data is sourced from Yahoo Finance and may be delayed. Nothing generated by this tool constitutes financial or investment advice.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features (2026 Update)

The dashboard organizes four key market perspectives into a unified 2x2 grid:

1. **Open Interest vs. Option Volume by Expiration (Top-Left)**
   * Compares total Open Interest (bar chart) against Call and Put Volume (dual-axis line plots) across upcoming expiration dates to highlight where liquidity and institutional positioning are concentrated.
2. **Today's Price vs. Net Sentiment (Top-Right)**
   * Tracks intraday stock price action alongside normalized net options sentiment (`Calls - Puts`). Dynamically color-codes bullish (`Calls > Puts`, green) and bearish (`Puts > Calls`, red) flow throughout the trading session.
3. **Top 10 Call Strikes (Bottom-Left)**
   * Displays the highest-volume call strikes paired with their Open Interest and a dashed reference line marking the current **Spot Price** to identify potential resistance walls or gamma magnets.
4. **Top 10 Put Strikes (Bottom-Right)**
   * Displays the highest-volume put strikes paired with their Open Interest and the current **Spot Price** marker to identify potential support floors and hedging activity.

## 📈 Evolution: 2025 vs. 2026

* **2025 (Initial Prototype):** Built from scratch to explore pulling raw public options data via `yfinance` and plotting basic volume, open interest, and strike distributions.
* **2026 (Enhanced Dashboard):** Refactored and expanded (with AI-assisted development) to tell a cohesive story—combining intraday sentiment with multi-expiration structure, dual axes, spot-price reference lines, and cleaner visual hierarchy.
